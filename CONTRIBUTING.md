# Contribution Guidelines

This library was developed specifically for use in the [VirtualBuddy](https://github.com/insidegui/VirtualBuddy) app.

Contributions that are not related to VirtualBuddy will not be accepted. Feel free to fork and maintain your own version if you’d like to use the library for other purposes.

Contributions related to VirtualBuddy are governed by the [VirtualBuddy contribution guidelines](https://github.com/insidegui/VirtualBuddy?tab=contributing-ov-file), including its [LLM usage policy](https://github.com/insidegui/VirtualBuddy/blob/main/LLM_POLICY.md).
